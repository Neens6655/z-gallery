## 10. Visual Direction Notes

### What Changed from Current Design
1. **Max-width tightened** from 1440px → 1200px — more gallery, less corporate
2. **Section padding increased** from 96px → 120px — art needs breathing room
3. **Typography scale expanded** — added Display (72px) for manifesto, larger hero
4. **New semantic colors** — edition status (olive/gold/brick) with soft background variants
5. **New components** — edition badge, filter chip, email modal, remix chain, toast, collection card
6. **Removed** — pricing cards, frame previews, shopping cart, chatbot (stays but deprioritized)
7. **Manifesto typography** — left-aligned, oversized, with thick border framing (Bauhaus poster style)

### Design Principles for This Project
- **The art is the hero** — every layout choice gives the SVG artwork maximum space
- **Scarcity signals are subtle** — edition badges inform, they don't scream. Think gallery placards.
- **Actions feel premium** — "Claim this seed" should feel like acquiring art, not clicking "Add to Cart"
- **Bauhaus DNA preserved** — 2px borders, 0 radius, terminal shadow, IBM Plex Mono, cream/charcoal
- **System, not decoration** — consistent tokens, no one-off styles, everything from the palette
