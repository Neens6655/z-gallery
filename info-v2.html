About ZSIGNAL — Computational Art

Vera Kohl / ZSIGNAL is a computational artist based in the intersection of algorithmic systems and Bauhaus principles. With background in generative art, mathematical composition and deterministic rendering, ZSIGNAL creates artworks where every seed produces a unique, reproducible artifact.

ZSIGNAL opens a playground for creation, experimentation and multidisciplinary intersection, fusing deterministic algorithms with historical art movements to develop visual stories with mathematical precision. 31 canonical works. 7 eras. Every render is inevitable.
