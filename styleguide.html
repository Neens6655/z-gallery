Z-Gallery Style Guide

The visual language of a deterministic generative art system. Bauhaus DNA — IBM Plex Mono, 2px borders, zero radius, cream & charcoal, gold accents.

48px height, 2px borders, gold focus state. Used in claim modal, print request, and contact forms.

Email Capture Modal

Claim flow modal. Backdrop blur, slide-up animation, gold submit button. Feels like an acquisition, not a checkout.

12

Toast Notification

Fixed-bottom confirmation messages. Charcoal background, cream text, slide-up, auto-dismiss after 3s.
