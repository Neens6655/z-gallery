Archive — 31 Deterministic Artworks

Vera Kohl / ZSIGNAL is a computational artist exploring the intersection of algorithmic systems and Bauhaus principles. Working with deterministic generative engines, ZSIGNAL creates artworks where every composition is a reproducible, signed artifact.
ZSIGNAL opens a playground for creation, experimentation and multidisciplinary intersection, fusing deterministic algorithms with historical art movements to develop visual stories with mathematical precision. Every seed is a universe. Every render is inevitable.
