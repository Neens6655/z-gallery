The Rules

Six Bauhaus rules.
Zero exceptions.

01
Zero Border-Radius

Forms follow function. Every corner is a right angle. Rounding is decoration — Bauhaus eliminates decoration without purpose.

border-radius: 0
02
2px Solid Borders

The line is the structure. Two pixels. Always charcoal. The boundary between elements is the clearest form of communication.

border: 2px solid #1C1C1C
03
IBM Plex Only

Mono for headings, labels, and code. Sans for body text. The typographic system does not expand. Every character earns its place.

IBM Plex Mono · IBM Plex Sans
04
Terminal Shadow

A flat offset shadow — X, Y, blur zero. The z-axis is honest. Elements sit on a plane, not in a fog. No diffusion, no illusion.

6px 6px 0 rgba(28,28,28,0.12)
05
Gold Is Decoration

#D4A84B fails WCAG AA on cream. It exists only for marks — borders, dots, icons. Never as readable text. #A67C32 for text use.

#D4A84B (deco) · #A67C32 (text)
06
Deterministic Art

The engine is a pure function. No random state. No drift between renders. The same four inputs always produce the same SVG. Art is math.

f(archetype, palette, seed, density) → SVG
