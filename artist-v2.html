The Collection

Seven Eras

Each era explores a different archetype of compositional logic, inspired by the master artists of geometric abstraction.

Philosophy

The Algorithm as Brush

A ZSIGNAL artwork is not drawn. It is computed. The artist defines the system of constraints: 12 geometric primitives, 6 palettes derived from art history, 7 compositional archetypes. The seed number becomes the artist's signature, the density their brushstroke pressure.

Every composition is deterministic. The same parameters always produce the same artwork. This is not a limitation. It is a guarantee. In a world of generative noise, ZSIGNAL offers generative truth.

The engine draws from Moholy-Nagy's transparency, Mondrian's grids, Klee's rhythms, Lissitzky's tension, Albers' color interactions, and the infinite tessellations of Islamic geometry. Seven eras. Seven conversations with the masters.
